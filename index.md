## About Me

I am a master's student in Natural Language Processing at **Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI)**, supervised by **Xiuying Chen** and **Le Song**. My research focuses on **AI for Science**, particularly agents and foundation models for bioinformatics and healthcare.

I want to build systems that help scientists reason over biomedical evidence, connect discovery with machine learning experiments, and make reliable, interpretable decisions. Previously, I worked as an Applied Scientist intern at Inception and an AI Engineer at Kalbe Farma. I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science from Universitas Indonesia.
